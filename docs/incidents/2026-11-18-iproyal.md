# 18 November 2026 — intermittent IPRoyal latency and timeouts

The fixed IPRoyal IPv4 on SOCKS5 port 12324 was intermittently responding too
slowly for normal use. The watcher last confirmed the expected route at about
13:59 for Claude and 14:00 for OpenAI, Novosibirsk time. The user switched away
from personal Config routing during diagnosis. Later watcher observations of
other IPs do not establish an ISP address change.

## Measurements

- With Blanc Amsterdam and Almaty, VPN-only Cloudflare probes completed in
  about 0.45–0.57 s. Adding the IPRoyal exit produced four 10 s timeouts;
  extended attempts completed in 13.5 s and 22.1 s with the assigned IP.
- On the same Amsterdam entry, IPRoyal took 12.8 s for OpenAI and 18.6 s for
  Anthropic. The other commercial ISP exit completed those requests in about
  1.25 s. An IPRoyal Cloudflare request timed out during authentication.
- At about 08:11 UTC, three paired fresh Cloudflare requests per route gave:

  | Route | IPRoyal median (range) | Proxy-Seller median (range) |
  | --- | ---: | ---: |
  | Physical interface directly to the proxy | 11.69 s (10.75–13.38) | 1.29 s (1.23–1.34) |
  | Via Blanc Amsterdam | 10.77 s (8.29–19.20) | 1.39 s (1.34–1.44) |

  In one direct IPRoyal sample the SOCKS5 authentication wait alone was 6.46 s,
  before the destination hostname was sent.
- Around 08:18 UTC, a 1 MB Cloudflare download through IPRoyal timed out after
  30 s on the first attempt. The second took 10.36 s to first byte and 12.54 s total.
- Around 08:28 UTC, four of nine support-listed SOCKS5 ports timed out. The
  original 12324 took 2.74 s and 11.61 s. The two fastest alternatives on the
  first pass took about 10 s on repeat. Successful probes returned the assigned IP.

The same symptom occurred without Shadowrocket's routing and via multiple paths.
This points to the particular IPRoyal endpoint or network segment near it.
The tests do not establish whether the proxy node, upstream route, or another
shared component is at fault. The announced 17 November maintenance window was
for the dashboard and ended about 23 hours before the observed incident.

## Support and recovery

Ask IPRoyal network engineering to inspect the endpoint and upstream path and
restore service on the same assigned IP. Support suggested port alternatives and
asked for a screenshot. A real crop of Shadowrocket's **Edit Server** page was
prepared showing type, address and port, with credentials outside the frame.
Keep the assigned IP as the acceptance condition. An occasional fast response or
a successful IP check alone does not establish recovery.

## Controlled direct baseline at 10:09–10:12 UTC

The initial direct rerun completed 5/5 Cloudflare traces in 2.49–2.68 s.
The extended run used sequential fresh SOCKS5 connections to the original
port from one Mac, bound to the physical interface, with no Blanc hop.
Each successful trace required verified TLS, HTTP 200 and the assigned exit.
Six traces per host succeeded without timeouts.

| Host | Median | Range |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Cloudflare | 2.51 s | 2.45–2.73 s |
| OpenAI | 2.64 s | 2.55–3.05 s |
| Anthropic | 2.52 s | 2.39–2.63 s |

SOCKS5 authentication took 0.26–0.31 s and the CONNECT reply took
0.52–0.55 s after authentication. Three 1 MB curl downloads also succeeded
in 3.97–4.20 s, with first byte in 2.22–2.33 s.

Support observed more source addresses than the order's four-IP allowance.
Multiple VPN egress addresses can be visible to IPRoyal even from a single
device. The direct run shows a reproducibly healthy path during this window.
It does not establish what the provider counted as active or whether it
enforced the limit at the earlier failure timestamps. Ask support to compare
the source IP count and limit events in the two windows. Confirming recovery
of the personal Shadowrocket chain requires a separate native check.

## Zurich chain and Xeovo entries at 10:54 UTC

An isolated client used the saved Zurich entry and the current IPRoyal SOCKS5
endpoint. All 18 short HTTPS requests returned the assigned IP. The six trials
per target took 2.96–3.63 s (Cloudflare), 2.93–3.16 s (OpenAI), and
2.82–3.13 s (Anthropic). The first 1 MB download series produced two successes
at 4.31–4.37 s and one 18 s timeout with zero bytes received. This shows that
Zurich can carry the chain, but the run does not establish stable downloads
or voice support in Shadowrocket.

The five saved Xeovo entries were checked without adding any IPRoyal sessions.
New York succeeded 8/8 and returned one distinct public address, with median
total time 1.13 s. Germany Trojan, Sweden and Singapore failed both short HTTPS
attempts. The saved Netherlands SS hostname returned NXDOMAIN from two DNS
resolvers. These are results for the saved nodes and current access network.
New York is a candidate for a later isolated chain test. Its short sample does
not establish a permanent egress IP or compatibility with IPRoyal.

Private reports: `zurich-chain-175424.private.json` and
`xeovo-only-175430.private.json` under `out/diagnostics/2026-11-18/`.
